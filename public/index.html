What you
actually get

Four things, end to end. From the whiteboard to the App Store — and the metrics after.

Strategy

Product clarity in weeks, not quarters

A fuzzy ambition becomes a roadmap the whole company can see: positioning, product logic, the shape of the thing three years out. Founders use it to raise; teams use it to stop arguing.

Systems

Design systems that let forty ship like four

Tokens, components, design-to-code pipelines. Built so the next hire is productive on day two and the next redesign never happens.

Mobile

iOS products that feel native because they are

HIG-grade interfaces with real motion, real gestures, real constraints — prototyped in code before engineering ever sees a ticket.

AI

Interfaces where the model is a material

Assistants, copilots, agentic flows. Designed for trust: what the model knows, what it’s doing, and how to stop it.

The best interface is the one you stop noticing. The best product is the one you can’t.

I’ve led design for banks, enterprise SaaS, marketplaces and AI products — inside organisations with thousands of people, and inside startups where I was the whole design team. I work where product, business and engineering overlap, and I’m most useful when the brief is still a question. I’m a design engineer: I design in Figma and in code, and I ship.
