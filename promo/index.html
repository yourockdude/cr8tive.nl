Food delivery · Spain

Brand identity · Mobile app · Campaign

Cocina de tu barrio

Good food from around the corner.

A neighborhood-first food delivery concept that brings independent kitchens together in one simple mobile experience. The work spans market research, brand identity, app design, packaging, and a campaign that takes the brand into the street.

Scope
Research · Identity · Mobile app · Packaging
Category
Food delivery

01 / The opportunity

Built around the neighborhood.

We started with the idea of bringing trusted local kitchens closer to their neighbors. Research helped shape a clear proposition for discovering, ordering, and growing beyond a single district.

02 / The app

From discovery to doorstep.

A direct path through restaurant discovery, dish search, checkout, and live order updates. Clear steps keep the experience easy to follow before and after the order.

03 / The brand

Made to live beyond the screen.

A bright, confident identity extends from the app to packaging, shopfronts, delivery concepts, and transit campaigns—meeting people where their neighborhood already is.

A connected brand and product experience for local food, from the first browse to the final handoff.
